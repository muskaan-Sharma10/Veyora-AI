How it works

From upload to interview in one workspace

  1. 01

    Describe the role

    Paste a job description or pick a saved job. The Job Description Analyst extracts required and preferred skills.

  2. 02

    Upload resumes

    Drop PDF, DOCX or text resumes. The Resume Intelligence Agent structures every candidate profile.

  3. 03

    Press Review Resume

    Seven agents run a real backend workflow with visible progress — parsing, scoring, gaps, and interview plans.

  4. 04

    Shortlist and invite

    Compare candidates side by side, reserve a slot, preview the invitation and send it.
